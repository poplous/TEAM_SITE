Charging plans that keep up with real life.

EVFlow is an online scheduler for shared EV charging sites. It re-plans as cars arrive late, leave early or ask for more energy, and it never breaks the site’s power limit.

See the projectMeet the team

The project

Universities, offices, apartment complexes and fleet depots share a limited amount of charging power among drivers with different deadlines. A schedule made once goes stale quickly.

The problem

Vehicles arrive late, leave early, change their energy needs or receive less power than expected, and a fixed plan can’t absorb that.

Our approach

A rolling-horizon scheduler re-plans at fixed intervals from the live site state, with a stated fairness rule and explicit commitments to each driver.

How we’ll prove it

Reproducible experiments in ACN-Sim against baselines, measuring unmet energy, delay, cost, peak demand, fairness and replanning time.
